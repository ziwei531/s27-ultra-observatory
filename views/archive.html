03 / Editorial standards

Uncertainty belongs
in the story.

We link to reporting, separate original tips from repetition, and keep disagreements intact. A rumor repeated by five sites is not five independent confirmations.

What do the states mean?

Reported: a sourced allegation, not established fact. Disputed: conflicting accounts or an explicit challenge. Superseded: retained for history with a linked replacement. Confirmed: backed by official evidence. Empty states are intentional, not missing content.

How is confidence assigned?

Low: one rumor chain, indirect interpretation or speculation. Medium: specific attributed reporting or inspectable evidence, still unconfirmed. High: independent primary corroboration. These labels are editorial judgments, not percentages. Read each note’s reasoning.

What changes each week?

Each weekly review attempts fresh research. Material evidence gets a new dated snapshot; an unchanged week gets only a review record. Old snapshots stay intact. Scheduling is external to this website; the visible last-check date is the record of completed work, not a promise that a timer ran.

Where does the artwork come from?

Optical study 01 is original abstract artwork, released under CC0. It is neither a leaked device image nor an authentic Samsung render. Publisher images remain at their original source links.
